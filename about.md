## Our Mission

Simulated Intelligence is dedicated to solving general-purpose embodied AI for the physical world through simulation and sim2real transfer. We're building intelligent agents that can help humans accomplish meaningful tasks across diverse robotic platforms.

## Founder

**Miguel Alonso Jr** founded Simulated Intelligence to advance the frontier of embodied AI and robotics. With a passion for building practical AI systems that bridge the gap between simulation and reality, Miguel leads our research and service initiatives.

Connect with Miguel on [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/miguelalonsojr).

## Where We Are

Founded in **Miami, FL**, Simulated Intelligence operates with a global distributed team model. We believe the best talent is everywhere, and we're committed to building a collaborative environment that transcends geographic boundaries.

## Our Values

### Human-Centered AI

We create AI and embodied intelligence for the benefit of mankind. Our focus is on augmenting human capabilities to increase productivity, not replacing them. We don't believe in replacing engineers with LLMs or physical labor with humanoids—we believe in empowering people with intelligent tools that enhance their abilities and free them to focus on higher-value work.

### Building in the Open

We're committed to transparency and building in the open. Our research, insights, and learnings are shared with the broader community to advance the field of embodied AI.

### Open Source

We believe in the power of open source to accelerate innovation. We contribute to and support open source projects that push the boundaries of robotics and AI.
